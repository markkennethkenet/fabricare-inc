# FabriCare study objectives: where each one lives

This maps each objective to the code that implements it, how it is evaluated, and what the results mean.
Measured numbers come from `model/v4_training_report.json`, written by the V4 training notebook.

## 1. Compare CNN, GLCM + Decision Tree and GLCM + SVM for fabric identification

- **Training and comparison:** [`docs/fabricare_v4_training_colab.ipynb`](fabricare_v4_training_colab.ipynb)
  (generated by [`scripts/build_v4_notebook.py`](../scripts/build_v4_notebook.py)).
  - Data: the audited, garment-grouped V3 split (train / validation / test) plus 292 verified polyester
    photos added to training only. Test photos are used once, in the final comparison.
  - CNN: MobileNetV2, EfficientNetV2-B0 and ConvNeXt-Tiny, each trained with retake-style augmentation
    (flip, small rotation, zoom, shift, brightness and contrast). The backbone with the best validation
    macro-F1 is exported.
  - GLCM: the scanner's exact 72 texture features ([`model_runtime.extract_glcm72_features`](../model_runtime.py)),
    with Decision Tree and SVM settings chosen on validation macro-F1.
- **Reported metrics:** accuracy, macro-F1, per-class precision/recall/F1 and confusion matrices on the
  held-out test photos; accuracy and label consistency on simulated retakes; agreement on 11 phone photos
  of one cotton garment. The production V3 CNN is evaluated on the same photos as a baseline.
- **In the app:** the scan page's *Choose Fabric Identification Model* card offers all three, with each
  model's test accuracy and a *Recommended* badge on the CNN.

**V4 results (November 2026 run).** 2,690 training photos (V3 train plus 292 polyester additions), 711 validation,
353 test. Retakes are four simulated re-captures of each test photo (new crop, light level, tilt and JPEG
compression). "Consistency" is the share of retakes that keep the clean photo's label. The phone set is 11 photos of
one cotton garment.

| Fabric model (test split) | Validation accuracy | Test accuracy | Test macro-F1 | Retake accuracy | Consistency | Phone (cotton) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Previous production CNN (MobileNetV2 V3 refit) | 72.85% | 81.87% | 0.822 | 76.63% | 84.9% | 9/11 |
| MobileNetV2 V4 | 74.26% | 83.00% | 0.831 | 81.09% | 92.9% | 8/11 |
| EfficientNetV2-B0 V4 | 75.67% | 84.42%¹ | 0.842¹ | 83.14%¹ | 94.3%¹ | 5/11 |
| **ConvNeXt-Tiny V4 (selected, deployed)** | **77.50%** | **86.69%**² | **0.863**² | **84.42%**² | 90.1% | **9/11** |
| GLCM + SVM V4 | — | 59.49% | 0.590 | 49.65% | 65.4% | 11/11³ |
| GLCM + Decision Tree V4 | — | 55.52% | 0.497 | 43.70% | 66.4% | 11/11³ |

CNN rows use mirror averaging except ¹ (single view scored higher). ² The deployed 16-bit form of the selected model;
the 32-bit original scored 86.40% (macro-F1 0.860), and the one-photo difference comes from rounding. ³ With 55–60% test
accuracy, 11/11 on one cotton garment most likely reflects a lean toward predicting cotton, not reliable
identification. The CNN was selected on validation macro-F1 alone; test photos were read once, for this table.

Mirror averaging on its own did not change the previous CNN's accuracy (81.87% → 81.59%, one photo). The gain comes
from retraining with retake-style augmentation: +4.8 points on clean test photos and +7.8 points on simulated retakes.
- **Re-measure on new photos:** [`scripts/evaluate_fabric_scans.py`](../scripts/evaluate_fabric_scans.py).

## 2. Compare Decision Tree, Random Forest and SVM for care recommendations

- **Features** ([`care_model.py`](../care_model.py)): identified fabric (one-hot), detected condition
  (YOLOv8 hole, needle mark, pilling and stain counts and box sizes), colour features (luminance, saturation,
  dark-pixel share) and a GLCM texture summary.
- **Labels:** the laundry mode from `care_model.reference_laundry_mode`, built from the sources the app cites
  (Woolmark, UGA Extension, ISO 3758, GINETEX, Levi's denim care, Cotton et al. 2020). Five modes: machine
  normal warm, machine gentle cold, hand wash cold, professional cleaning, repair first.
- **Evaluation:** garment-grouped V3 splits; test accuracy and macro-F1 with the true fabric and end to end
  with the fabric predicted by the selected CNN.
- **Limitation to state:** accuracy measures agreement with the referenced rules, not laboratory-verified
  care outcomes. A garment's care label always takes precedence in the app.
- **Damage thresholds:** on the mostly undamaged V3 photos, the YOLOv8 detector reported holes on 35.1% of photos at a
  40% score but 3.4% at 90%. Automatic care, health and lifespan effects therefore need hole ≥90%, stain ≥70%,
  pilling ≥70% or needle mark ≥60% (`care_model.DAMAGE_MIN_CONFIDENCE`); weaker findings stay visible for the user
  to review, and confirmed findings always count. The detector itself (`model/best.pt`) is a short baseline and
  would benefit from retraining with more annotated damage.

**V4 results.** Labels on the test split: machine gentle cold 132, hand wash cold 117, machine normal warm 94,
repair first 8, professional cleaning 2.

| Care model | Validation macro-F1 | Test accuracy (true fabric) | Test accuracy (CNN-identified fabric) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decision Tree (selected, deployed)** | **0.999** | 99.15% | 90.65% |
| Random Forest | 0.997 | 99.43% | 90.93% |
| SVM (RBF) | 0.918 | 95.75% | 87.82% |

The near-perfect true-fabric scores show how faithfully each algorithm reproduces the reference rules. The end-to-end
column, which includes the CNN's fabric errors, is the realistic figure for the app.

## 3. Mobile-responsive app with fabric confidence and condition assessment

- Android, iOS and web builds from one Flutter code base. Web scans upload photo bytes and save history in
  the browser (IndexedDB); phones save photos in app storage.
- Smart camera ([`lib/screens/scan/smart_capture_screen.dart`](../lib/screens/scan/smart_capture_screen.dart)):
  live light, motion, focus and distance guidance with auto-capture and cropping to the guide box.
- Result screen: large fabric name with the model score, the other likely fabrics, condition findings and
  damage review. All three fabric models report a confidence score (SVM uses Platt-scaled probabilities).

## 4. Fabric health assessment and lifespan estimation

- **Health score** ([`assessment.health_assessment`](../assessment.py)): 0–100 from the visible findings, with
  a grade (Excellent / Good / Fair / Poor). Confirmed findings count fully and candidates about half. The point
  weights are FabriCare assumptions.
- **Remaining lifespan** ([`assessment.lifespan_estimate`](../assessment.py)): a range in months built from
  Laitala and Klepp (2020), *Sustainability* 12(21):9151, §4.1. The expected total lifespan is about 5.2 years;
  relative to cotton, silk lasts 2.2 years longer, wool 1.2 years and synthetics 5.5 months. Ten more wears per year
  shorten lifespan by about 2 months, and washing less often adds up to 4.8 years. The garment's age is subtracted.
  Visible damage reduces the estimate (FabriCare assumptions), and a confirmed opening caps it at 6 months until
  repaired. Because the survey model explains about 22% of the variation, the app shows a ±40% range.

## 5. AI laundry-mode suggestions and automated care-risk alerts

- Every scan returns `ai_care` ([`fabricare_api.ai_care_for`](../fabricare_api.py)): the laundry mode from the
  trained care model (objective 2) with its confidence, the referenced reasons, and alerts for openings, stains,
  pilling, colour bleeding, felting, water marks, heat sensitivity and dye transfer, each with sources.
- The result screen shows these in the *AI laundry suggestion* card. Without a trained model, or for an
  uncertain fabric, the reference rules are used and labelled as such.
